Physical Reminders

A coin to carry hope in your pocket

Tokens designed to inspire and remind their holders of purpose, for someone in a hard season, or for you as you set out to encourage others.

Front Side

Features the Chi Rho, an ancient symbol of Christ, representing His name in Greek, alongside the Alpha and Omega, symbolizing that God is the beginning and the end, always present and eternal.

Back Side

Engraved with the words of Joshua 1:9, offering strength and encouragement for the road ahead:

"Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go."

This is our first edition, antique bronze, about 1.38 inches across. The font size on the back is smaller than we intended; future editions will fix that while keeping the same heartfelt purpose.
