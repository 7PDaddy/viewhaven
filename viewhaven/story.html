Our Story

Where the name
ViewHaven came from

At ViewHaven, we believe everyone deserves to feel valued, find their purpose, and know they are never alone. Our mission is twofold: to directly inspire hope and encouragement through meaningful reminders, and to empower others to extend that same encouragement to the people around them.

A Legacy of Generosity

ViewHaven was born from a legacy of generosity and resilience. The name is a tribute to my father, Robert Dolan, and his nursery, Viewhaven Nursery, in Damascus, Maryland. His work with evergreens, a symbol of growth, renewal, and strength, represents the enduring values that guide this mission today.

My dad, out on a run.

One wintry morning in 1995, my dad went out for a run he wouldn't normally take, on a road he wouldn't normally choose, to encourage a friend who was trying to get back in shape. He was struck by a car. The driver was sixteen, and it seems she may have simply lost focus for a moment. He never woke up. He was 51 years old.

I'm 54 now. Three years older than he ever got to be.

That morning matters here because of what he was doing when it happened. He wasn't out there for himself. He was out there to encourage someone else. In a small way, that's what ViewHaven is trying to keep doing. I wrote more about that moment and what it changed in me in The One Thing That Changed Everything for Me.

This initiative is also inspired by the kindness of my family. My mother, Molly Parezo, my stepmother, Sharon, and my stepfather, George, each lived lives of service and giving, shaping me into who I am. Their influence encouraged me to give back and create something meaningful that honors their memory.

The name "ViewHaven" has been with me for nearly 20 years, but its purpose has only now become clear. It's not just a place for offering encouragement, it's a platform for equipping others to share that encouragement with their own communities.

What We Do

Through ViewHaven, we focus on creating reminders, both physical and emotional, that inspire and encourage. The goal isn't only to reach those who feel lost or alone, but also to empower others to join in spreading hope and purpose.

We aim to bridge the gap between those who need encouragement and those who are ready to give it. Whether through a simple coin, a shared story, or a moment of kindness, the tools here are meant to multiply acts of encouragement far and wide.
